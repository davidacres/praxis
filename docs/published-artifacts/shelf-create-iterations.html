Ten iterations on starting a note or a session from the shelf

Each round takes the one before it, names the weakest thing about it, and fixes that. Every round shows the same measures, so you can see whether it actually improved. All frames are at real app scale, with the rail fixed at 14px and the card at 340px.

  • Glyphs blur at 1×.Ribbons and 1.4px strokes on an off-grid 12-unit box turned soft once shrunk to 9px.
  • Tiny targets.02's 12px tiles were the clearest, but a 12×12 target is too small to hit reliably.
  • Cards need a name.Only 05's coloured header band said "new session" or "note" at a glance.
  • Sideways 7.5px text fails.05's labels could not be read at real size.
  • No fake agent lines.02's pre-written greeting looked like a model reply that never happened.
  • Nobody said what happens after send.Every round-one card stopped at the send button.

07

Full-width cells that become the card's edge

fixes 06Each action fills the whole 14px rail width as a 24px tinted cell, which doubles the target area. The open cell fills solid, and the card slides out level with it so its coloured edge continues from the cell. The card now reads as pulled from that button.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 1stays in shelf after send no
  • Better: target area doubled for 7px more rail height than 06; the card's origin is obvious.
  • Weakest point: you still click into the field, and the card asks you to read three blocks before you can type.
08

Type first

fixes 07Focus lands in the goal box at the top of the card. Starters are a short list under it that disappears once you type, and ↓ selects one. Agent, permission and project shrink to one summary chip beside send. The title comes from the goal, so there is no title field. The note becomes one field whose first line is its title.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf after send no
  • Better: nothing to click before typing; the card shrinks to a goal, one chip and a send button.
  • Weakest point: sending still throws you onto the full session page. The point of the shelf is to stay where you are.
09

It stays a session

fixes 08After you send, the same card turns into the shelf's own session preview, and a new spine slides onto the shelf, lit while the agent works. When the agent replies, the card shows the reply and a quick-reply box. You only leave the page if you choose to.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf after send yes
  • Better: new, preview and reply are now one card in three states; the shelf is a complete loop.
  • Weakest point: the rail glyphs are still the round-one set. Look closely at the cells and they are soft at real size.
10

Pixel-fit glyphs

fixes 09Both glyphs are redrawn on a 10×10 pixel grid with 1px strokes centred on half-pixels, so every edge lands on whole pixels at 1×. The session glyph is a speech bubble with a plus inside; the note glyph is a page with a folded corner and two lines. The tint already says "new", so the shapes stay simple.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf yesglyph edges on pixel grid 100%
  • Better: crisp at Compact in both themes; the plus and the page lines no longer smear into the outline.
  • Weakest point: a glyph still has to be learned. Nothing says "New session" until a slow title tooltip appears.
11

The button says what it does

fixes 10Hovering or focusing a cell slides a label flag out of the rail at the cell's height, with the same motion as the cards. It replaces the slow title tooltip without adding 05's 128px of sideways text. Keyboard focus draws its ring inside the cell, and an unsaved note shows a dot on its cell.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf yeslabel shown 120ms, not ~1s
  • Better: anyone can tell what the buttons do on first hover, and the flag carries live state ("unsaved draft", "in Praxis").
  • Weakest point: the card ignores where you are. With a ticket open, the starters are still generic.
12

Knows where you are

fixes 11The card picks up the page behind it. With PRX-503 open, a removable ticket chip sits in the band and the starters are about that ticket. A note opened from the same page is linked to the ticket, and one click unlinks it.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf yescontext re-typed none
  • Better: the starters answer what you're actually looking at, and notes file themselves.
  • Weakest point: it is now very easy to start a second session about something you're already discussing in one.
13

Don't start twice

fixes 12While you type, a quiet row offers today's session that matches by ticket or title. ⌘↵ adds your message to that session and ↵ still starts a new one. The note's goal button becomes a split button with the same choice.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf yesduplicate sessions offered an alternative
  • Better: the shelf gathers related work into one session rather than scattering it across several.
  • Weakest point: every frame so far is the happy path. What does the card do with no provider, or when the send fails?
14

Holds up off the happy path

fixes 13Each failure gets a designed state inside the same card, and your text is never lost. With no usable provider, the send button becomes a setup link. When a send fails, the error sits above your text with Retry. The dark palette is checked separately, because amber on dark needed its own text colour.
rail 59pxtarget 14×24clicks before typing 0stays in shelf yesstates designed 6 of 6
  • States covered: empty, typing, sending, working, waiting for reply, failed. Plus no provider and note save error (today's "Couldn't save · Retry" kept).
  • Weakest point: it's now fourteen good ideas in fourteen places. It needs one spec somebody can build.
